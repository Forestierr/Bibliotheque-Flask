Milestone of 100 Classes Reached: Thank You for Your Trust!

Reading time: 3 min | By Robin Forestier, creator of Livres en classe

Two years ago, Livres en classe was just a small weekend project designed to lend a helping hand to my mother, an elementary school teacher, who was spending way too much time manually writing down book loans in loose-leaf notebooks.

Today, we have just crossed a meaningful milestone that is very close to my heart: more than 100 teachers and classrooms now rely on Livres en classe every day across Switzerland, France, and Canada.

1. From a Weekend Idea to a Tool Shared Among Colleagues

The story began very simply in front of a tall stack of books on a classroom desk. Faced with the weekly chore of misplaced index cards, misspelled book titles, and untraceable loans, a straightforward question arose: why should managing a classroom reading corner be so tedious when we all carry a smartphone in our pocket?

The initial goal was modest: create an ultra-simple web tool requiring no complicated installation, allowing a teacher to scan an ISBN barcode in two seconds and assign it to a student's name. Two years later, this family project resonated warmly with dozens of colleagues and schools seeking a humane, intuitive, and advertising-free classroom tool.

2. A Few Heartwarming Numbers

Behind this 100-user milestone, there is above all a vibrant classroom life taking shape with daily reading routines:

+10,000

Books catalogued in your classroom libraries

~1,000

Autonomous students borrowing, reading, and discovering stories

+1,000

Loans and returns managed with a quick barcode scan

Knowing that this tool helps you free up precious time for what matters most — nurturing a genuine love of reading in your students — is the greatest possible reward.

3. A Project Growing Through Your Feedback

If the platform has come this far today, it is first and foremost thanks to you. Your suggestions for improvement, encouraging feedback, and word-of-mouth recommendations among colleagues in staff rooms have shaped the application to match the practical realities of everyday classroom life.

A huge and heartfelt thank you to our "founding members" and to everyone who adopted the platform early on, tested new features, and shared invaluable pedagogical advice.

4. What's Next on the Roadmap?

Development is actively moving forward! Upcoming updates will make your daily classroom routine even easier:

  • Enriched search and children's book cards: ongoing improvements to youth book search and detailed metadata (curated summaries, reading level indicators).
  • Printable labels and tracking sheets: new options for book bin labels, barcode sheets, and printable reading logs to empower students and foster reading accountability.
  • Shared resources and reading guides: practical guides and shared pedagogical resources for designing and running welcoming classroom reading corners.

5. Final Words

Thank you once again for your enthusiasm and renewed trust. Happy reading to all of you and your students!

Robin

Creator of Livres en classe
