Archiving Your Classroom at the End of the Year: Why It Beats Deleting Everything

Reading time: 4 min | By Robin Forestier, creator of Livres en classe

Every June and September, teachers encounter the very same question: what should I do with my student roster from the past year and their loan history?

Out of habit or lack of time, many choose to delete all students with one click to "start fresh". However, that means losing months of valuable progress. Annual archiving was built specifically to solve this puzzle: start with a clean slate while preserving and celebrating everything your students accomplished.

1. The Trap of Radical Deletion

As summer holidays begin, it is tempting to head over to Settings and click "Delete all students". In an instant, the class list is wiped clean and ready for the next cohort.

Yet by doing this, you permanently erase:

  • Reading leaderboards: who read the most books? Which students made tremendous breakthroughs in reading independence?
  • Your classroom's hit books: which titles were checked out 25 times? Which novels sat untouched and might need to be highlighted differently or replaced?
  • Pedagogical records: no objective figures left for school reports, reading assessments, or parent-teacher conferences.

2. Why Deleting Data Is a Missed Opportunity

Classroom reading is not simply an inventory workflow; it is an educational journey. Erasing past records is like tossing out the ship's log at the end of the voyage.

Furthermore, if a child forgot to return a book before the holidays, deleting their profile also removes the active loan. The book disappears from your radar, making it nearly impossible to trace in September.

3. The 4 Big Advantages of Annual Archiving

Instant Quota Reset

Archived students no longer count toward the 40-student limit of the free plan. You start your next school year with 0 active students without losing any history.

Reading Highlights & Top Readers

Automatic calculation of key class metrics: total books read, class average per student, most popular book, and the podium of top readers.

Safeguard Against Lost Books

The wizard checks for outstanding loans before closing. If a student is staying in your multi-grade class next year and keeping a book for the summer, you can easily keep them active.

Printable A4 Summary

Generate and print a handsome annual certificate and class summary sheet for your teaching portfolio or classroom display.

4. How the 3-Step Wizard Works

In Livres en classe, we built a simple, step-by-step assistant located in your Account Settings:

  1. Step 1: Check Current Loans. The wizard alerts you if any books are still checked out. Settle returns with a single click or flag students keeping a book over summer.
  2. Step 2: Annual Summary & Statistics. Review your classroom reading achievements: total loans, books per student, and the top 3 favorite books of the cohort.
  3. Step 3: Secure Closing & Fresh Year. Seal the cohort under a clean label (e.g. 2025-2026). Your dashboard is instantly refreshed, ready for your new students.

5. Celebrating Milestones: Diplomas & Continuity

Archiving also turns the end of the school year into a rewarding celebration. With your archive numbers in hand, you can award students their customized Super Reader Diplomas or validate their reading passports.

Teaching a multi-grade classroom or looping with your students? You can reactivate an archived student at any moment with their entire reading log intact.
